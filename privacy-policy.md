# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Kuqo respects your privacy and is designed to operate without collecting or storing personal information.

## Camera Access

The App requests access to your device’s camera solely for the purpose of scanning QR codes or barcodes. Camera access is used in real-time and no images or video are recorded, saved, or transmitted.

## Use of Scanned Data

The App uses the scanned codes (e.g., barcodes or QR codes) as **keys to request information from third-party systems**, specifically:

- **e-Kasa (Slovak Financial Administration system)** – for retrieving purchase data from receipts
- **Open Food Facts API** – for translating barcodes into grocery names and related product data

The scanned codes are **not stored or retained**. They are only used temporarily to make these external requests during active use of the app.

## Data Collection and Storage

- The App **does not collect or store any personal data**.
- No scanned data, user information, or API responses are saved on the device or uploaded to any server.
- The App does not use analytics, advertising, or any background tracking mechanisms.

## Third-Party Services

While the App communicates with third-party systems (e-Kasa and Open Food Facts), it does not share any personal data, nor does it store or log communication with these services.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App’s functionality changes. Updates will be reflected here, and we encourage users to review this policy periodically.
